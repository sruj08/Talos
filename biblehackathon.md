# Part 3 — Why Each Problem Statement Exists

## D1 — AI Agents & Agentic Commerce

- **What it asks:** Build AI agents that autonomously discover products, negotiate, and execute transactions using PayPal's MCP server and Agent Toolkit.
- **What it really wants:** PayPal wants the **Trust, Policy, & Escrow Control Plane for Agentic Commerce**. Look closely at `@paypal/agent-toolkit` (`github.com/paypal/agent-toolkit`): `create_order` is hardcoded to `intent: "CAPTURE"` with zero pre-execution policy firewall, zero `intent: "AUTHORIZE"` hold support, and zero `authorizationsVoid` rollback if a merchant fails delivery! PayPal needs a reference architecture (`AegisPay`) proving that autonomous agents can shop across **Channel3's** 100M+ products and pay via **PayPal** *without* prompt-injection or rogue hallucinations draining the user's wallet.
- **Indian trigger:** **RBI Digital Payment Security Controls**, **DPDP Act 2023** (verifiable consent artefacts for delegated actions), and **NPCI's 2025–2026 Agentic/Delegated Circle norms** requiring explicit human-in-the-loop step-up approval above configurable spending thresholds.
- **Global trigger:** The **Stripe ACP vs. PayPal ACS** platform war, **EU AI Act (Aug 2026)** Article 14 human oversight for high-risk automated financial actions, and **CFPB / Regulation E** liability questions around unauthorized agentic spend.
- **Organiser/sponsor alignment:** Hits **100% of the jury simultaneously**: Karthik Ravi & Himraj Singh (PayPal MCP + AI guardrails), Marco Podien (`Orders v2` Authorize/Capture/Void), Sylwia Vargas (**AG Grid** real-time agent policy & audit console), Mats Bryntse (**Bryntum Scheduler** 29-day authorization hold & shipment SLA timeline), Ignacio Valdez Bicard (**Channel3** product discovery), Ameer Hassan (**APIMatic** `@paypal/paypal-server-sdk` + custom Policy SDK), Carly Richmond (**Elastic** audit log), Pooja Mistry (**Postman** MCP collection), and Shifra Williams (**Render** Blueprint).
