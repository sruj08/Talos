## Round 2 — Aggressive Attack & Veto Round

**CHARLIE MUNGER †:** How does `CivicTrace Escrow` die? Two ways: First, a crooked contractor uploads a stock photo of solar panels downloaded from Google Images to trick your Vision LLM into releasing the PayPal Payout! Second, you violate India's **FCRA (Foreign Contribution Regulation Act, 2010)** if you route foreign PayPal donations into an unlicensed domestic NGO bank account!

**ANDREJ KARPATHY & MIKKO HYPPÖNEN (Defeating Fake Photo Proofs):** Great catch on fake photos. We enforce a **3-Layer Multimodal Proof Protocol**:
1. **Cryptographic Challenge Token in Frame**: When Milestone #2 opens on **Bryntum Gantt**, `CivicTrace` generates a dynamic 4-digit visual/QR challenge code (or requires live timestamp + GPS EXIF + sun-angle shadow consistency check).
2. **PayPal Shipment Tracking Cross-Check**: For physical equipment milestones, the supplier's carrier tracking number must be registered via `@paypal/agent-toolkit`'s `create_tracking` / `get_tracking` tool and show `DELIVERED` in the target postal code `[S3]`.
3. **Channel3 Price-Inflation Check**: Before Milestone #1 is ever approved, `CivicTrace` queries **Channel3** (`trychannel3.com`) for the exact equipment SKU to prove the contractor isn't billing $400 for a $95 solar battery `[S9]`.

**HARVEY SPECTER ◇, BLYTHE MASTERS & DR. R.S. SHARMA (Solving FCRA / 501(c)(3) / Escrow Legality):**
- To stay 100% clean under **India's FCRA 2010**, **Income Tax Act s.80G**, and **US IRS 501(c)(3)** rules:
  1. For domestic Indian community co-ops (RWAs/CSR), inflows and vendor settlements stay strictly domestic or route through **Direct Supplier Procurement** (the donor's PayPal `Orders v2` payment buys the verified equipment directly from the merchant or authorized fiscal sponsor via PayPal, rather than pooling unrestricted foreign cash in an unregistered NGO account) `[S4, S13]`.
  2. Every disbursement row in the public **AG Grid Social Audit Ledger** exports a statutory **Form 10BE (80G) / 501(c)(3) / CSR-2** audit trail, with personal beneficiary PII redacted under **DPDP Act 2023** `[S7, S14]`. Legal veto cleared!
