## 1. Main Devpost Page Verbatim Summary & Key Sections

### Headline & Tagline
- **Title**: PayPal AI Hackathon
- **Tagline**: Build the Next Generation of Commerce with PayPal and AI
- **Format**: Online Public Hackathon
- **Registered Participants**: 24,754 participants
- **Total Prizes**: $69,750 in prizes across 24 prize slots ($67,500 cash + $2,250 in Render credits)

### About the Challenge
Build the Next Generation of Commerce with PayPal and AI. Whether you are building autonomous shopping agents, reimagining peer-to-peer money movement, streamlining small business operations, elevating consumer checkout, or driving social impact, the PayPal AI Hackathon invites developers globally to build applications combining PayPal's commerce infrastructure (`@paypal/agent-toolkit`, PayPal MCP Server, `@paypal/paypal-server-sdk`, PayPal JS SDK v6, Orders v2, Invoicing, Subscriptions, Disputes, Shipment Tracking, Payouts) with modern AI capabilities and partner developer tools.

### Suggested Domains / Tracks
1. **AI Agents & Agentic Commerce**: Autonomous shopping, negotiation, agent-to-agent (A2A) payments, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, and programmable spending controls.
2. **Money Movement Experiences**: Next-generation P2P transfers, group bill splitting, cross-border settlements, micro-payouts, and conversational money movement.
3. **Business Solutions**: Merchant automation, invoicing, subscription management, dispute resolution, milestone escrow, and multi-party marketplace payouts.
4. **Consumer Experiences**: Smarter discovery, price protection, personalized checkout, BNPL (Pay Later), family/senior protection, and post-purchase experiences.
5. **Social Impact Ideas**: Transparent charitable giving, community mutual aid, disaster relief payouts, financial accessibility, and climate/cooperative funding.

### Requirements (What to Build & What to Submit)
- **What to Build**: A working software application that integrates PayPal developer tools/APIs (such as PayPal AI Toolkit / Agent Toolkit, PayPal MCP Server, PayPal Server SDK, or PayPal JS SDK v6) with AI.
- **What to Submit**:
  1. **Project Description**: Detailed Devpost write-up explaining what the project does, how PayPal and partner tools were integrated, and the architecture.
  2. **Demo Video**: Publicly visible video hosted on **YouTube** (under 3 minutes) demonstrating the working application.
  3. **Public Code Repository**: Link to a public **GitHub** (or open-source repository) containing the full source code created during the submission period.
  4. **Open-Source License (MANDATORY STAGE 1 GATE)**: Must include an open-source license file (`LICENSE`, e.g., MIT or Apache 2.0) that is **visibly displayed in the top "About" section of the repository**.
  5. **Working Prototype Link**: Accessible link to test the live application (plus Sandbox credentials/instructions).

## 2. Official Rules Verbatim Extract (`https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules`)

### Dates and Timing (Official Rules §2)
- **Submission Period**: Begins October 1, 2026 (9:15 am PDT) and ends **November 12, 2026 (12:00 pm PST)** [which corresponds to **November 13, 2026 @ 1:30 am IST (GMT+5:30)**].
- **Early Eligibility Check Deadline**: Submit by **November 5, 2026** for Devpost early screening (allows fixing missing license/video/repo issues before final close).
- **Judging Period**: December 1, 2026 (12:00 pm PST) – December 15, 2026 (5:00 pm PST).
- **Winners Announced**: On or around December 21, 2026 (12:00 pm PST).

### Eligibility & Team Rules (Official Rules §3)
- Open to individuals who have reached the age of majority in their jurisdiction of residence (at least 18 years old) and organizations (up to 50 employees).
- **Geographic Exclusions**: Void in Crimea, Cuba, Donetsk, Luhansk, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Russia, Belarus, Venezuela, or any country subject to US sanctions, and where prohibited by law. (**India, US, UK, EU, Canada, Singapore, Australia, Brazil, etc. are fully eligible**).
- **Team Size**: Unlimited team size (1 or more eligible individuals; teams appoint 1 Representative to submit and receive/distribute prize funds).

### Project Requirements & Third-Party Integrations (Official Rules §4)
- Projects must be **newly created** (or substantially modified—must disclose any pre-existing code) during the Submission Period.
- **Financial / Banking Warning**: Projects must use **test / sandbox data only** (PayPal Developer Sandbox) and must **not** require judges to link real bank accounts, real credit cards, or live financial credentials.
- **Maximum Submission Limit**: Each participant or team may submit a **maximum of two (2) distinct projects**.

### Judging Process & Criteria (Official Rules §6)
- **Stage 1 (Pass / Fail Screening)**:
  - Devpost screens all submissions for baseline viability, adherence to project requirements, working YouTube video (<3 min), public repo with visible open-source `LICENSE` in the top About section, and substantive integration of PayPal tools.
- **Stage 2 (Scored Evaluation by Judges — 100% total, 5 Equal Criteria at 20% each)**:
  1. **Technological Implementation (20%)**: Quality of software development, depth of PayPal + AI integration, architectural soundness, and effective leverage of partner tools.
  2. **Design (20%)**: Thoughtfulness of user experience, visual polish, intuitiveness, and human-factors design.
  3. **Potential Impact (20%)**: How big of an impact the solution can have on target users, merchants, or the broader commerce ecosystem.
  4. **Innovation / Idea (20%)**: Creativity, originality, and how uniquely the solution addresses a real-world problem.
  5. **Presentation (20%)**: Clarity and effectiveness of the demo video and written documentation.
- **Tie-Breaking Order (Official Rules §6)**: In the event of a tie between eligible submissions, the tie will be broken based on the higher score in the Criteria **in the exact order listed above**:
  1. Technological Implementation (1st tie-breaker)
  2. Design (2nd tie-breaker)
  3. Potential Impact (3rd tie-breaker)
  4. Innovation / Idea (4th tie-breaker)
  5. Presentation (5th tie-breaker)

### Prizes & Prize Stacking Cap (Official Rules §8)
- **Grand Prizes**:
  - **1st Place**: $12,000 USD (1 winner)
  - **2nd Place**: $8,000 USD (1 winner)
  - **3rd Place**: $5,000 USD (1 winner)

- **Sponsor Prizes ($17,500 USD cash + $2,250 Render credits across 16 slots)**:

    - 2nd Place: $2,000 USD (1 winner)

  - **Best Use of APIMatic**: $1,000 USD + 6-month APIMatic Business Plan subscription each (3 winners)
  - **Best Use of Bryntum**: $1,000 USD each (3 winners)
  - **Best Use of Channel3**: $1,500 USD (1 winner)

    - 1st Place: $1,000 Render Credits (1 winner)

- **PRIZE STACKING RULE (Official Rules §8)**:
  - A single submission may win **at most one (1) Grand Prize OR one (1) Honorable Mention**, **PLUS at most one (1) Sponsor Prize**.
  - Therefore, a project **cannot** win both a Grand Prize and an Honorable Mention, and **cannot** win multiple Sponsor Prizes.
  - **Maximum Possible Cash Payout per Project**: **$17,000 USD** ($12,000 1st Place Grand Prize + $5,000 Best Use of AG Grid 1st Place).
  - If a team submits two (2) distinct projects (the maximum allowed under §4), the second project can target a second combination (e.g., $5,000 Honorable Mention + $1,500 Channel3 or $1,000 Bryntum/APIMatic).
